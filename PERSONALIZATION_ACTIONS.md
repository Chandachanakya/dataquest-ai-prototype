# Personalization Action Mapping

This document outlines the decision table that maps each computed learner state to a set of concrete, actionable personalizations within the DataQuestAI platform. This serves as the blueprint for the application layer to trigger the right actions based on the output of the `learner_states` table.

| Learner State | Rule Trigger (Why?)                                                               | Personalization Action                                                                   | System Goal        |
| :------------ | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :----------------- |
| **Drop-risk** | Prolonged inactivity or a sharp decline in login frequency.                       | 1. **Trigger Re-engagement Quest:** A simple, high-value quest ("Log in and complete one lesson for 100 bonus XP!").<br>2. **Send Peer Nudge:** Notify a study buddy (if applicable): "[User]'s streak is in danger! Send them a nudge to keep going."<br>3. **Deliver Summary Email:** "Here's what you've missed on DataQuestAI..." | Re-engage & Retain |
| **Struggling**  | Low quiz scores, high number of attempts on the same content, minimal XP gain.    | 1. **Assign Foundational Revision Quest:** Automatically assign a quest focusing on prerequisite concepts for the difficult topic.<br>2. **Recommend Forum Post:** Prompt with: "Struggling with [Topic]? Ask the community on the forum!"<br>3. **Suggest AI Mentor Session:** "Ask our AI Mentor to explain [Topic] in a different way." | Remediate & Support |
| **Fast**        | High quiz scores, low time spent per module, rapid XP accumulation.               | 1. **Unlock Advanced Challenge:** Offer an optional, difficult "boss fight" or a coding challenge related to the topic.<br>2. **Promote to Competitive Leaderboard:** Place the user on a "Top Performers" weekly leaderboard to encourage competition.<br>3. **Offer Course Skip:** "You're flying through this! Would you like to test out of this module and move to the next one?" | Challenge & Retain |
| **Consistent**  | Steady progress, no risk indicators, meeting platform-defined engagement norms.   | 1. **Normal Progression:** No special intervention needed. Continue with the standard learning path.<br>2. **Offer Social Quests:** Propose collaborative quests, like "Complete a lesson with a study buddy."<br>3. **Positive Reinforcement:** Award badges for consistency (e.g., "10-Day Streak!", "Module Master"). | Nurture & Motivate |
